usage: train_packaged_models.py [-h] --output OUTPUT [--promote]

Refit supported renal models from the packaged cohort and its fixed patient
split. Outputs are staged by default. --promote copies only passing research
artifacts; it never changes clinical rules, service routes, authorization or
raw source data. The previously evaluated patient split is reused, not a new
independent test set.

options:
  -h, --help       show this help message and exit
  --output OUTPUT
  --promote

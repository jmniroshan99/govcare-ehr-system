Main behavior
- Patient Identification now includes a ward selector.
- A selected ward limits identification results to active admissions in that ward.
- Ward patient count, available-bed count, current bed, and admission number are displayed.
- QR/barcode searches respect the selected ward.
- The Bed Board opens with the selected ward pre-filtered.
- Age is calculated from date of birth using the current date.
- Date of birth overrides a stale stored age.
- Existing ages are backfilled by Flyway V13.
- Future dates of birth are rejected.
- Queue, pharmacy, duplicate checking, patient PDFs, and clinical PDFs use dynamic age.

The previous Pharmacy Verify/Reject workflow fixes are retained in this package.
